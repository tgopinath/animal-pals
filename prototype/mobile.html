Mobile app

Animal Pals on a phone

A field guide for kids: real photos, real recorded calls, video and 3D models of animals, birds and every other living being.

  • Tap Hear on any card for a real recording from the wild.
  • Press and hold a photo to watch a short video.
  • Search by typing, or tap the mic and say a name.
  • Play the quizzes: guess who's calling from a sound, or name the animal in a photo.
